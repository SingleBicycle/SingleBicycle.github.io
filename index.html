Zihao (Zack) Zhu

I am an incoming Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Texas A&M University, co-advised by Prof. Zhiwen Fan and Prof. Gaurav Pandey. I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Texas A&M University.

My research aims to develop trustworthy and uncertainty-aware AI systems that can perceive, reconstruct, and reason about the visual world, with applications in spatial intelligence, robotics, and embodied AI.
